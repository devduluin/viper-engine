## P1: approval snapshots could change after digest validation

Proposal/revision retained caller-owned arguments/snapshot during access-check awaits. Mutating
them after digest validation could persist an approver view different from the bound digest.

Fixed: snapshot/freeze proposal arguments, tool snapshot, binding and revision arguments before
I/O, using existing JSON/tool parsers. Later checks and inserts share that exact immutable input.
The in-memory session now imports the same APPROVAL_ARGUMENTS_DIGEST constant as durable stores.

## P1: connection-ready alone did not mean the server remained accessible

Approval admission/consumption checked connection auth_state, but ignored disabled servers and
private-server ownership changes. Fixed: join and lock both server and connection; reject disabled
servers and private servers belonging to another actor. Refusal preserves approval/call state
and creates no dispatch attempt.

## P1: a backdated decision could bypass expiry before the sweeper

Proposal, acceptance, consumption and amendment now use the later of trusted caller time and
database time sampled after locking the run. Past proposal/replacement deadlines are refused.
An expired proposal cannot execute just because its sweeper has not yet updated the status.
